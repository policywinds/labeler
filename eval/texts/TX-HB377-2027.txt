H.B. No. 377
A BILL TO BE ENTITLED
AN ACT
relating to an expiration date extension for a license to carry a
handgun following renewal materials submission.
BE IT ENACTED BY THE LEGISLATURE OF THE STATE OF TEXAS:
SECTION 1. Subchapter H, Chapter 411, Government Code, is
amended by adding Section 411.1851 to read as follows:
Sec. 411.1851. EXTENSION OF LICENSE EXPIRATION DATE
FOLLOWING RENEWAL MATERIALS SUBMISSION. (a) The expiration date
of a license is extended to the 90th day after the expiration date
as printed on the license if, on or before the 10th day before the
printed expiration date, the license holder submits by mail or on
the Internet the renewal materials described by Section 411.185(a).
(b) On the license holder's submission of the renewal
materials under Subsection (a), the department shall promptly
provide to the license holder by mail or electronically, as
applicable, a receipt indicating the extended expiration date of
the license.
(c) For purposes of Chapters 30 and 46, Penal Code, and the
other laws of this state, a person who receives an extension under
this section is considered to hold an unexpired license for the
additional period permitted under Subsection (a) if the person
carries both the receipt described by Subsection (b) and the
person's expired license while the person carries a handgun.
SECTION 2. The change in law made by this Act applies only
to a handgun license that expires on or after the effective date of
this Act.
SECTION 3. This Act takes effect September 1, 2027.
